A week away from everything that keeps you busy

MahaDevi sits near the Putumayo river, in a cooler part of the jungle with few mosquitoes. You sleep in a cabin, eat meals prepared for you, and spend your days between the river, the waterfall and the ceremony house.

The ceremonies follow the Camsá tradition and are led by a healer who grew up in it. Around them, a team that has supported more than 400 ceremonies looks after your safety, your preparation, and what you do with the experience once you're home.

Understanding a ceremonial retreat

What it is

A multi-day program built around traditional ceremony. It starts with screening and preparation weeks before you travel, and continues with integration support for three months afterwards.

How this one differs

The ceremonies are led by an Indigenous healer in his own region, not by a visiting facilitator. Groups stay small, and the team is large enough that someone is always near you.

Who it's for

Adults who want time to slow down and reflect, including first-timers. It is not a medical treatment, and not everyone is accepted. Screening decides whether it's a safe fit for you.

Questions people ask first

Anything else, ask us on your call or on WhatsApp.

Is this suitable for a first-timer?

Yes. Many guests have never sat in ceremony before. Small groups, screening before acceptance, a gentler daytime ceremony, and one staff member for every two guests are all there to support people who are new to this.

How do you keep guests safe?

Every guest is screened medically and psychiatrically before being accepted. Trained facilitators are present throughout each ceremony, and a hospital is about 30 minutes away.

Is it legal?

The retreat takes place in Putumayo, Colombia, where these Indigenous ceremonies are a long-standing tradition and MahaDevi operates within the local legal framework. We're glad to go through the details on your call.

How much does it cost, and what does that cover?

Retreats start at $3,550 per person in a shared room and $3,850 in a private cabin. The price covers lodging, meals, ceremonies, airport transfers, preparation and 90 days of integration care. Flights are not included.

How long is the retreat?

Seven nights and eight days, with four ceremonies during the week.

What happens after I go home?

You have 90 days of integration care with our team, to help you make sense of the week and carry it into daily life.
