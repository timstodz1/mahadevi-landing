Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information MahaDevi Retreats ("we", "us") collects through mahadeviretreats.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website only.

What we collect

InformationWhere it comes from
Your name, email address and phone or WhatsApp numberYou type them into the enquiry form
Your answers to the form's three questions: what you're looking for, whether you've attended a ceremony before, and when you'd like to comeYou choose them in the enquiry form
The page address you sent the form from, the time you sent it, and any advertising tags in that address (such as a Google Ads click ID or campaign name)Added automatically when you send the form
Your IP address, browser type and device informationSent automatically by your browser to the services that run this site, listed below

We don't ask for health information through this website. Medical and psychiatric screening happens later, only if you decide to apply, and it is covered by the information we give you at that stage.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your enquiry and set up the free call you asked for.
  • To follow up about retreat dates, availability and preparation, if you've asked about a retreat.
  • To understand which of our ads and pages lead to enquiries, so we can improve them.
  • To keep records we need for legal, tax or safety reasons.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone so they can market to you.

Services we use

These companies process information on our behalf to run this site and handle enquiries:

  • LeadConnector (GoHighLevel) stores your enquiry in our contact management system and sends our follow-up messages.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and receives standard web server information, such as your IP address, when you load a page.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this site, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Google Ads measures whether a visit from one of our ads led to an enquiry. It uses cookies and similar technology to do this.
  • WhatsApp handles any messages you send us through the WhatsApp buttons, under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Some of these companies are based in the United States, so your information may be processed there as well as in Colombia.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google Ads to measure how our advertising performs. When you arrive from one of our ads, Google may set cookies or read a click ID so we can count enquiries that came from that ad. We may also use this information to show our ads to people who have visited this site.

You can control personalized ads in Google's ad settings, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The enquiry form still works if you block cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we're in contact about a retreat, and for up to three years after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or the law requires us to keep them longer.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you, and give you a copy
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop sending you messages

You can also reply "stop" to any message, or use the unsubscribe link in any email. Depending on where you live, including in the European Union, the United Kingdom, Canada, California or Colombia, you may have further rights under local law, such as the right to complain to your data protection authority. We will respond to requests within 30 days.

Security

This site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and the services above protect the information they hold. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our retreats are for adults only. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
